I received my **M.S. in ECE** from the **Georgia Institute of Technology**. My research broadly explores **AI agents and autonomous systems**, with a particular interest in building agents that can operate, learn, and collaborate over extended periods of time. My previous research spans multimodal large language models and multimodal understanding, including work on emotion recognition and reasoning. Alongside my research, I build open-source tools for developers and AI agents and am part of the **GitHub Maintainer Advisory Network**.

My current research interests include:

1. **Agent–Harness Co-evolution, RSI & Agentic Systems**
2. **General-Purpose & Long-Horizon AI Agents**
3. **Persistent Memory, State, and Continual Adaptation**

I'm interested in making AI useful in everyday work, and I build open-source tools to put that into practice. I created **[Understand Anything](https://github.com/Lum1104/Understand-Anything)** <a href="https://github.com/Lum1104/Understand-Anything"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Lum1104/Understand-Anything?style=social" alt="GitHub stars" style="vertical-align: middle; display: inline;"></a> to help people and AI agents make sense of complex codebases through static analysis, knowledge graphs, and LLM-based reasoning. I also develop **[dsh-browser](https://github.com/omdsh-dev/dsh-browser)** <a href="https://github.com/omdsh-dev/dsh-browser"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/omdsh-dev/dsh-browser?style=social" alt="GitHub stars" style="vertical-align: middle; display: inline;"></a>, which connects **DeepSeek Harness** to the browser so agents can help people complete tasks on the web, working within their existing tabs and login sessions.
